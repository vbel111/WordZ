Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · applies to the Word Z app on iOS and Android

The short version: Word Z has no ads, no sign-in and no tracking. Your progress is saved on your device. When you buy something, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google and recorded by our purchase provider, RevenueCat, so it can be restored. We don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Who we are

Word Z ("we", "us") is the developer of the Word Z: Hex Word Search Puzzle app. You can reach us at support@wordz.game.

What we collect and why

DataWhere it staysWhy
Game progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, Word Book, settings)Only on your deviceSo the game remembers where you are.
A random player ID (for example K7Q2…)On your device; shown in support emails you sendGives each player their own level boards and helps us find your purchases if you contact support. It isn't linked to your name, email or device identifiers.
Purchase history (product, date, price, store transaction ID) and an anonymous app user IDApple / Google and RevenueCatTo deliver what you bought, restore it on a new device and prevent fraud.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising ID or browsing history, and we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. Purchase records are processed by RevenueCat, Inc. on our behalf, under its own privacy policy, only to validate and restore purchases.

Game engine

Word Z is built with Unity. The Unity engine may send anonymous technical information about your device (such as device model, operating system version and graphics hardware) to Unity Technologies to help improve the engine. See Unity's privacy policy.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game opens your phone's share sheet with a short text you can send. Nothing is shared unless you choose to send it.

Children

Word Z is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no ads, chat or user profiles. Purchases are protected by Apple's and Google's parental controls (for example Ask to Buy and Family Link).

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Game progress: delete it any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, or by deleting the app.
  • Purchase records: email us your player ID (in the support email the game creates for you) and we'll ask RevenueCat to delete the records linked to it. Apple and Google keep their own receipts under their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. Email support@wordz.game and we'll reply within 30 days. We don't sell personal information.

Security

Progress stays on your device and purchase data is sent over encrypted connections to Apple, Google and RevenueCat.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in the game.
