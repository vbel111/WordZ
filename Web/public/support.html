Support

We usually reply within 2 business days.

Contact us: email support@wordz.game. The fastest way is Settings → Help & Support in the game: it opens an email that already includes your player ID, so we can find your purchases.

Frequently asked questions

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game, then go to Shop → Restore Purchases. If it's still missing, email us with your player ID and the date of purchase. Your store receipt (from Apple or Google) helps too.

How do I get my Supporter Pack on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device. iPhone and Android device backups usually bring it along when you set up a new phone from a backup. There's no account sign-in yet.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store. On iPhone, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

How do I unlock more categories?

Earn stars by finishing levels (up to 3 per level). Each locked category opens when your total stars reach its goal, or right away with gems.

How do I turn daily reminders on or off?

In the game, go to Settings → Daily Reminder. You can also turn off notifications for Word Z in your phone's settings.

A word I found wasn't accepted

Each level has its own hidden words, and extra real words count as bonus words. If you think a common word is missing, email it to us and we'll look at adding it.

Does Word Z have ads?

No. Word Z has no ads at all. Coin and gem packs are optional.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app. To delete purchase records too, email us your player ID. See the Privacy Policy for details.
